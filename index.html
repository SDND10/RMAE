About

Meet the team.

Rocky's the kind of town where you like to know who's working on your car. Call RMAE and you talk to the team who'll actually be under the bonnet. No call centre, no middleman.

FAQ

Things people ask us.

Not covered here? Call or text 0427 667 996. A two-minute chat usually sorts it.

What vehicles do you work on?+

Commercial and light vehicles: everyday cars, utes, 4WDs, vans, light trucks and fleet vehicles for local businesses. If you're not sure yours fits, call and ask. It probably does.

Can you come to me if I can't get the car in?+

Yes. Most jobs run through the workshop at Kent Street, but the service truck is set up for electrical, aircon and diagnostic work on the spot, at home, at work, or wherever the vehicle has stopped, across Rockhampton and Central Queensland. If the job needs the hoist or a longer stint on the bench, we'll bring it back to the workshop.

My battery keeps going flat. Can you find out why?+

One of the most common calls we get. We test the battery and charging system, check the alternator and look for anything drawing power when the car is switched off. You get a straight answer on what's actually wrong before anything gets replaced.

Do you do air conditioning?+

Yes: diagnosis, leak detection, repairs and regas, in the workshop or from the truck. Blowing warm, smelling odd or only working sometimes are all things we see every week.

Can you fit a dual battery or solar setup?+

Yes. Dual battery systems, solar charging, electric brake controllers, driving lights and other accessories, wired properly so they keep working on corrugations, not just in the car park.

Do you do regular servicing and mechanical repairs too?+

We do. Servicing and mechanical repairs sit alongside the electrical work, so you're not shuffling the car between two shops to get one problem sorted.

How do I book?+

Call or text the team on 0427 667 996, or use the form at the top of the page. Tell us what the vehicle is and what it's doing, and we'll come back to you with a time and a rough idea of cost.
